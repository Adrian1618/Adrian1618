## Adrian Gutierrez, Ph.D.

Computational and experimental physicist specializing in detector physics, scientific computing, and data-driven analysis.  
Former lead analyst for the Electromagnetic Calorimeter (ECAL) within the MUonE experiment at CERN.

My work connects experimental measurements with simulation, signal processing, and statistical modeling. I develop methods for reconstructing detector signals, correcting noise, calibrating sensor response, and validating results against physical expectations. My research also includes machine-learning classification and image-processing studies for particle physics.

### Research Expertise

### Professional Experience

**MUonE — CERN / University of Virginia**
- Led ECAL calibration, reconstruction, and detector performance studies.
- Developed common-mode noise correction and iterative gain-matching methods.
- Synchronized independent detector streams and validated integrated Tracker–ECAL reconstruction.
- Compared experimental measurements with simulation to characterize energy and spatial resolution.

**CMS — CERN / University of Virginia**
- Simulated magnetic-monopole signatures using GEANT4.
- Developed analytical trajectory models and studied calorimeter timing and pulse observables.

**ATLAS — CERN / University of Oregon**
- Investigated calorimeter feature extraction using Sobel edge detection.
- Evaluated TensorFlow deep-learning methods for particle classification in trigger studies.

**Duke University — Research Internship**
- Evaluated XGBoost, TMVA, and scikit-learn classifiers for supersymmetry searches.
- Compared classification performance and computational tradeoffs across simulated signal scenarios.
